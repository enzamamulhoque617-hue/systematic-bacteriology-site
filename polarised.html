10-MARK EXAM NOTES

Polarised Microscope

Write the answer systematically using the following headings.

01 • INTRODUCTION

Introduction

The Polarised Microscope is one of the microscope types included under microscopy in Unit V.

It is used to view materials such as crystalline and mineral materials.

02 • PRINCIPLE / KEY FEATURE

Principle

The Polarised Microscope uses polarised light for examining materials that show characteristic behaviour with polarised illumination.


The notes particularly associate it with the examination of birefringent substances.

03 • MATERIALS OBSERVED

What Can Be Viewed?

According to the handwritten notes, the Polarised Microscope is used to examine materials such as:

  • Crystals
  • Mineral granules
  • Cellulose fibres

The notes describe these in relation to birefringent substances.

04 • MAIN COMPONENTS

Important Microscope Components

The general microscope component section in your notes identifies the following parts:

01

Light Source

The light bulb or LED is located at the base and provides illumination.

02

Eyepiece

Present at the top and acts as the viewing lens through which the sample is seen.

03

Objective Lens

The primary lens that captures light and creates the initial magnified image.

04

Stage

The platform where the glass slide is placed for observation.

05

Support Stage Clip

The metal clip holds the specimen slide securely.

06

Fine Adjustment

Helps obtain a sharper and clearer image.

07

Diaphragm

Controls the light passing through the specimen.

08

Base

The heavy bottom part supports the microscope.

09

Arm

The supporting portion used to safely carry the microscope.

10

Stage Control

Used to adjust the stage position.

05 • WORKING

Working Concept

  • The specimen is placed on the stage.
  • Illumination is provided through the microscope.
  • Polarised light is used for examination.
  • The specimen is observed through the optical system.
  • Materials showing birefringence can be examined.
  • The eyepiece is used for viewing the specimen.
  • Fine adjustment helps obtain a clearer image.
