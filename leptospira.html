TOPIC 02 • SPIROCHETES

Leptospira

The handwritten notes describe Leptospira as a very delicate and actively motile spirochete, with characteristic spiral morphology and importance in human infection.

Group Spirochete
Nature Very delicate & actively motile
Specimens Blood / Urine

1. Morphology

  • The notes describe Leptospira as very delicate.
  • It is described as actively motile.
  • The organism has a characteristic spiral form.
  • The notes mention approximately 6–20 coils.
  • The ends are described as hooked.
  • The notes mention that the organism can be observed with a suitable microscope because of its delicate structure.
⭐ Morphology memory: Very delicate + actively motile + spiral + hooked ends.

2. Cultural Characteristics

  • The handwritten notes state that Leptospira can be grown in enriched media.
  • The optimum temperature mentioned is approximately 25–30°C.
  • The notes mention a pH of approximately 7.4–7.5.
⭐ Culture memory: Enriched media → 25–30°C → pH 7.4–7.5

4. Pathogenesis

The notes describe Leptospira infection as an infection that can affect humans.

The handwritten notes mention that the infection may produce clinical symptoms and that the disease can involve different tissues.

The notes specifically mention involvement of the kidneys and jaundice among the clinical/pathogenic features.
