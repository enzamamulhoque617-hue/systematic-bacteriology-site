TOPIC 04 • NOTES

Brucella

Brucella are small Gram-negative organisms discussed in the handwritten notes as important pathogens causing infection in animals and humans.

Type Small Gram-negative organism
Growth Slow / scanty growth
Importance Animal-to-human infection

1. Morphology

  • The notes describe Brucella as small Gram-negative organisms.
  • They are described as non-motile.
  • They may occur singly or in pairs.
  • The notes describe them as non-spore-forming.
  • The notes mention that they are non-acid-fast.
  • The notes also mention capsule characteristics.
⭐ Key morphology: Small + Gram-negative + non-motile + non-spore-forming.

2. Cultural Characteristics

  • Brucella are described as aerobic.
  • The notes mention that some species may require additional conditions for growth.
  • Growth is described as slow and scanty.
  • The notes mention incubation around 5–10 days.
  • On suitable media, colonies are described as small, moist and translucent.
  • The notes mention a greyish-white appearance of colonies.
Culture memory: Slow + scanty growth → small, moist, translucent colonies.

3. Biochemical Reactions

The handwritten notes mention the following biochemical reactions:

Test Reaction recorded in notes
Catalase Positive
Oxidase Positive
Urease Positive reaction is mentioned
⭐ Quick recall: Catalase + • Oxidase + • Urease +

4. Pathogenesis

The notes describe Brucella as highly pathogenic and associated with infection transmitted from animals to humans.

The notes mention infection associated with cattle and other animals.

They also mention exposure through infected animal material and consumption of contaminated milk / dairy products.

Infected animal → Exposure / contaminated products → Human infection

Clinical features mentioned

  • The notes mention fatigue.
  • Joint-related symptoms are mentioned.
  • Nervous-system involvement is also mentioned in the pathogenicity notes.

5. Laboratory Diagnosis

A. Specimen

The handwritten notes mention blood as an important specimen.

B. Culture

  • Blood culture is mentioned.
  • The notes mention incubation around 37°C.
  • Because growth may be slow, prolonged observation may be required.

C. Serological / Other Tests

The notes mention agglutination and immunofluorescence among diagnostic methods.

Diagnosis flow:
Blood → Culture → Identification → Serological tests
