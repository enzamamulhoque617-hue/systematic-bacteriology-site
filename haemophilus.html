Haemophilus

The handwritten notes particularly discuss Haemophilus influenzae, a Gram-negative organism associated with respiratory and other infections.

1. Morphology

  • Haemophilus is described as a Gram-negative organism.
  • The notes describe it as small.
  • It is described as non-motile.
  • It is non-sporing.
  • The notes describe a pleomorphic appearance.
  • The organism may occur as short bacillary or coccobacillary forms.
⭐ Key morphology: Small + Gram-negative + pleomorphic + non-motile + non-spore-forming.

4. Pathogenesis

The notes identify H. influenzae as an important human pathogen.

The notes state that H. influenzae was among the first strongly recognized organisms associated with disease in influenza.

The organism can be associated with respiratory disease and other infections.

H. influenzae → Human infection → Disease
